# Workshop electronic approval

New closures require an explicit electronic-signing declaration. The approving officer is resolved from the signed-in account and a unique active employee record, with fresh authorisation checked again in the closing transaction. Name, signing role, employee number, account UID/email, declaration and server approval timestamp are saved. Workshop managers sign in the Fleet Manager capacity unless a specific employee job title is recorded; the underlying employee role and department are retained separately. Super administrators retain their established allowlist access and sign as Super Administrator. No particular person is hard-coded.

The transaction saves the closed status, decision, an approval reference, a normalised snapshot of the reviewed job/decision/signing identity and its SHA-256 fingerprint, plus an approval audit entry on the same job document. A changed job card, switched signing identity or revoked employee access blocks signing before any fleet/defect/history writes. The existing vehicle, inspection-date, service and odometer updates remain in the transaction. The checksum is a record fingerprint, not a certificate-backed cryptographic signature.

The same sign-off block appears in Job Card review, the original popup printer, the same-page print override and the 90-day compliance report/PDF. Viewing a historical card makes no database changes. Already-closed cards display the original recorded approver/time as a Legacy approval record; missing role/identity stays Not recorded. No current name, role, signature or approval date is retrospectively inserted. Closed forms are read-only. Later alterations to a signed job's reviewed fields are identified against the stored snapshot.

Validate locally with `node --test tests/workshop-electronic-signoff.test.cjs`. Also test the actual signed-in manager account against the project's Firestore rules before deployment. Live rules are not in this repository and have not been verified: frontend/transaction validation does not establish server-enforced immutability, signature admissibility or legal compliance. ## Active employee lookup correction

Signing ignores inactive/deleted historical employee records that share the login email. Exactly one active canonical-email match is still required, with the existing Manager/Admin authorisation checks and transaction-time revalidation. Missing and multiple active matches are distinguished. If the exact Firestore email query returns no active record, a read-only directory lookup can verify older saved email casing/whitespace; it never matches by name, chooses between active duplicates, changes employee records or bypasses database permissions. Existing closed approvals, schema, signature payload and fleet update paths are unchanged.
